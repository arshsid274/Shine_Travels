Varanasi Trip Summary

₹189,25/Person

The 1-night, This 2-day Varanasi Bodhgaya tour takes you through spiritual landmarks of India. Begin with the ghats and temples of Varanasi, followed by the Ganga Aarti. Explore Sarnath, where Buddha delivered his first sermon. Visit Bodh Gaya, the Mahabodhi Temple, and the Vishnupad Temple in Gaya for a profound spiritual journey.

The 2-day tour offers a deep dive into India's spiritual heritage, starting with a visit to the sacred ghats and temples of Varanasi. Experience the serenity of Bodh Gaya, where Buddha attained enlightenment, and explore key Buddhist and Hindu pilgrimage sites like Sarnath, Vishnupad Temple, and Mahabodhi Temple.

Highlights

The tour highlights include visiting the sacred ghats and temples of Varanasi, where you can attend the iconic Ganga Aarti at Dasaswamedh Ghat. Explore Sarnath, the place where Buddha delivered his first sermon after enlightenment. In Bodh Gaya, visit the Mahabodhi Temple and the sacred Bodhi Tree. Finally, experience the Vishnupad Temple in Gaya, an important pilgrimage site for Hindus.

  • Experience the Vishnupad Temple in Gaya, dedicated to Lord Vishnu

Basic Information

Varanasi, one of the oldest cities in the world, holds immense religious and historical significance. Revered as the spiritual capital of India, it is known for its ghats along the Ganges River. Bodh Gaya, where Buddha attained enlightenment, and Sarnath, where he preached his first sermon, are key pilgrimage sites.

Tour Plan

  • Pickup from Varanasi Airport, Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS), Babatpur, Pindra, Uttar Pradesh 221006, India. Upon arrival, an Alkof Holidays representative will meet you and transfer you to the hotel in Varanasi. After check-in, relax at the hotel.
  • Later, explore the city, visiting various ghats, sacred temples, and the local market. In the evening, witness the Aarti ceremony on the banks of the River Ganges. Enjoy an overnight stay at the hotel.
  • After breakfast at the hotel, depart for Bodh Gaya, a significant Buddhist pilgrimage site where the Buddha attained enlightenment under the sacred Bodhi Tree. Upon arrival, check-in at the hotel.
  • Visit the Mahabodhi Temple, a revered place for Buddhists. Explore the Maha-bodhi tree, considered holy since Lord Buddha attained enlightenment beneath it. Afterward, visit Sujata Village, the Niranajana River, and various monasteries. Enjoy an overnight stay at the hotel.

  • After visiting the Vishnupad Temple, drive back to Varanasi. Upon arrival, check-in at the hotel for an overnight stay.
  • On the final day, check out from the hotel and transfer to Varanasi Airport for your onward journey. The tour concludes with fond memories of your visit.
